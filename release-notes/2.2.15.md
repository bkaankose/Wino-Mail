Wino Mail 2.2.15 connects your mail, calendar and To Do: you can turn a mail into a task or an event, set as many reminders as you need, and use repeating tasks, task reminders and Undo in To Do. This release also adds shared IMAP mailboxes, a redesigned account page and many calendar improvements.

## Highlights

_None_

## New and improved

- Turn mail into tasks and events: Right-click a mail or open the reader's More menu, then choose Create task (Ctrl+Shift+T) or Create event. Wino fills in the task or event from the mail and links them, so you can go from one to the other. You can also add a follow-up task from an event or schedule time for a task.
- Full control of reminders: Use Customize reminders to give an event any number of reminders, before or after the start, including email reminders on Gmail. Set a default reminder for each calendar in its account settings. Reminders changed in other apps now sync to Wino, and reminders now appear even when Wino is closed ([#1227](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1227), [#1235](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1235)).
- More powerful To Do: Tasks can now have reminders with Mark completed and Snooze, priority, categories, start dates and repeat schedules. Completing, deleting and moving tasks shows an Undo bar, and a new Flagged mail list under Important collects the mail you flagged in every account.
- Shared IMAP mailboxes: Folders that other people share with you appear under the owner's name, right below your pinned folders. They have a shared icon, and Wino offers only the actions your access allows ([#1202](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1202)).
- Manual sort, drag-and-drop ordering and the Completed and All filters now work in every To Do view, including My Day, Planned and Important, and all views share one manual order ([#1219](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1219), [#1238](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1238)).
- Copy calendar events and paste them on an empty time slot or day ([#1183](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1183)).
- Multi-day and all-day events now show as one bar across their days, with arrows in the title when an event runs over several days ([#1067](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1067)).
- Double-click a day in Month view to open it in Day view.
- The calendar shows the open tasks that are due on the days on screen in a row above the calendar, and you can tick a task there to complete it. You can turn this off in Settings > Calendar.
- When the Unread filter is on and marking mail as read on selection is off, a mail you mark as read now leaves the list ([#1232](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1232)).
- On Windows 11, you can now turn on Live Tiles for Start menus that show them, and pin Wino to Start from the Live Tile settings ([#1236](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1236)).
- When you print, you can now choose a page range and use every option your printer supports, together with Wino's margins, scale, background graphics and headers and footers ([#1242](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1242)).
- Copy a task list to another list or account with "Copy list to" in the list's options.
- Export task lists to iCalendar (.ics) or CSV files, and import tasks from those files.
- The Companion can now show every task due today or overdue: choose "Based on due date" in Settings > Companion. Its count now includes all of these tasks, and a "more" row opens any beyond the first three.
- The account page has a new design: each account lists Mail, Calendar, People and To Do with a switch for each. Each app has its own page, where you choose under Syncs with whether it uses a server or stays on this device. Sign-in and servers and Troubleshooting have their own pages.
- Outlook, Gmail and Exchange accounts can now keep Calendar, People and To Do on this device only.
- The account list and Personalization > App modes show which apps each account provides.
- In Sign-in and servers, each server shows its own test result, and Test connections now checks CardDAV servers too.
- Settings search now finds sender names and the People and To Do settings of each account.
- The Message List settings are grouped into sections.
- Wino now notifies you about new mail outside the Inbox when the notification scope is All folders.
- Deleting tasks no longer asks for confirmation, because the Undo bar can bring them back.
- When you create a task from a mail and the account has no task list yet, Wino offers to create one.
- The mail delete Undo bar now stays open for five seconds by default.
- Notification bars that have a button stay open for 10 seconds, close when you use the button, and work better with screen readers.
- The To Do sort menu marks the active sort.

## Fixes

- New IMAP and POP3 accounts now start with only Mail turned on, so you choose whether to add Calendar, People and To Do. The window's Back button now goes back one step in Mail server setup without resetting your choices ([#1243](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1243)).
- Special characters and emojis in IMAP subjects and sender names show correctly on servers that replace them ([#1207](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1207)).
- Free/busy changes on Google Calendar events now sync to Wino ([#1067](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1067)).
- "On the day" reminders of all-day CalDAV events now fire on the day of the event at the right time.
- Event details show the days of an all-day event instead of "12:00 AM - 12:00 AM".
- Wino now notifies you about new mail when your device's clock runs ahead of the mail server.
- Wino no longer notifies you again when another device moves a message to another folder.
- Dialogs keep working after you close the main window to the tray while a dialog is open.
- Mail server setup now uses server details as you type them, names the missing field, and says whether the incoming, outgoing (SMTP), CalDAV or CardDAV server failed.
- Accounts added through Advanced setup are now named after their email address unless you type a name, and Your name stays empty until you enter an address.
- Advanced setup asks which apps you want first when you open it before choosing them.
- Choosing Other (IMAP) or Other (POP3) as the provider now opens manual setup directly.
- Troubleshooting now says when an account has no protocol logs yet.
- The quick event popup opens empty and on the first click after switching to Month view. It stays inside the window, no longer opens over a dialog, and closes again with Escape. Today, Next and other commands now work on the first click while it is open.
- Choosing New event twice no longer opens two calendar pickers.
- An event no longer looks busy after its save finishes while the calendar is loading.
- Tasks from a deleted To Do list now leave My Day, Planned and Important and their counts right away.
- When you delete the open To Do list, To Do stays on that account's lists, and the account's lists stay open when you switch app modes.
- A deleted last To Do list that is kept on this device no longer comes back.
- The mail Undo bar no longer looks like an error, and screen readers now read its Undo button.
- Weekday names in the calendar's month picker stay on one line.
