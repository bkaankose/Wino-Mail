This update keeps your Gmail access tokens on your device when you use realtime push. It also fixes the privacy policy dialogs in small windows.

## Highlights

_None_

## New and improved

- Realtime push for Gmail no longer sends your mailbox access token to the push relay. Wino now proves your address with a sign-in token that cannot open your mailbox ([#1179](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1179)).

## Fixes

- The privacy policy dialogs for Realtime Push Notifications and Wino Intelligence now fit small windows, so you can reach the checkbox and accept the policy ([#1178](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1178)).
