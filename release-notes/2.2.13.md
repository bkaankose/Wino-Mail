Wino Mail 2.2.13 brings Wino Assistant, on-premises Exchange accounts, secure sign-in for IMAP accounts with a new setup that starts from your email address, Privacy & security settings and account groups. It also adds CalDAV To Do sync, answers to calendar invitations right in the message, and many fixes for IMAP, CalDAV and CardDAV accounts.

## Highlights

- **Wino Assistant.** Press a shortcut in any app, then type or say what you want done with your mail, such as finding, moving, replying or deleting, even several steps in one sentence. Wino lists its plan and changes nothing until you say Yes. Wino Assistant is part of Wino Intelligence; set it up in Settings > Wino Assistant.
- **On-premises Exchange and secure sign-in for IMAP.** Add your organization's Exchange Server account for mail, calendar, contacts and To Do. IMAP accounts now offer secure sign-in in your browser when your provider supports it, so you don't need an app password. For both, you type your email address and Wino finds the servers, also through Outlook Autodiscover ([#42](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/42), [#1184](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1184), [#1210](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1210)).
- **Privacy & security settings.** Choose to load everything, block trackers or block all remote content, allow or block senders and domains, remove tracking from links, and get a warning before risky attachments open, in Settings > Privacy & security ([#948](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/948)).
- **Account groups.** Linked inboxes are now account groups. Gather accounts under a named group with its own color and icon, then choose whether its folders are combined into one inbox or each account keeps its own folders, in Settings > Manage accounts.
- **Event invitations.** Calendar invitations show as a compact card with Accept, Tentative and Decline, also for forwarded invitations and IMAP accounts, and the mail list shows the event date ([#1220](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1220)).

## New and improved

- IMAP accounts sync To Do with their CalDAV server, and Outlook and CalDAV To Do now sync on the calendar schedule ([#1199](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1199)).
- To Do lists have a Manual sort, so you can drag tasks into your own order ([#1219](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1219)).
- POP3 accounts can send mail, and you choose under Messages on the server whether mail stays on the server, is removed after some days, or is removed when you delete it in Wino ([#703](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/703)).
- Attachments show as tiles with a colored file-type badge, the full file name and the size ([#1000](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1000)).
- You can mark unread messages as read when you move them to Trash ([#1203](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1203)).
- Forward is now available as a keyboard shortcut action ([#1221](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1221)).
- Deleting a recurring event asks whether to delete only this occurrence or the whole series ([#1212](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1212)).
- Smart Labels and Daily Briefing share one settings page, and the Wino Intelligence pages are simpler.
- Mail translation uses a new translation service and keeps emoji and links intact.
- Passwords and sign-in tokens are now kept in Windows protected storage. You don't need to sign in again.
- Calendar events sync on the calendar schedule, so changes made elsewhere arrive without a manual sync.
- Calendar invitations in the mail list use the same layout as other mails.
- Editing a CalDAV event or a CardDAV contact keeps every detail you didn't change, and contact photos from the server are imported.
- Accounts set up manually use the name from the email address as the sender name.
- Senders whose name starts with an emoji get readable initials.
- Screen readers announce folder names in folder customization.

## Fixes

- Mails are marked as read on IMAP servers that leave read changes out of their quick updates ([#1223](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1223)).
- A mail you read in an Unread list stays in the list while it is selected, instead of jumping to the next unread mail ([#1222](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1222)).
- All-day events show as "All day" in the Companion, widgets and the Live Tile ([#1224](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1224)).
- Special characters and emoji show correctly in IMAP subjects and sender names ([#1207](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1207)).
- Searches no longer get ignored while a previous search is still running ([#1187](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1187)).
- The navigation pane button opens the pane again when the window is narrow ([#1214](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1214)).
- The folder order you set by dragging in folder customization is saved ([#1201](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1201)).
- The unsubscribe message shows the correct sender after you switch to another mail ([#1200](https://github.com/bkaankose/Wino-Mail/issues/1200)).
- Empty folder and Mark folder as read act on every message on an IMAP server, new-mail notifications show the mails that actually arrived, and search works in folders that were never synchronized.
- New recurring CalDAV events appear right away, keep their time zone across daylight-saving changes, and their notes no longer show HTML tags in other apps.
- CalDAV To Do keeps shared calendars, recurring tasks, due dates and moved tasks intact.
- Calendar sync no longer fails with a sign-in error after the first contact sync on CalDAV and CardDAV accounts.
- Calendars sync at launch for accounts whose mail is still synchronizing.
- Previews of HTML-only mails no longer run words together.
- Wino opens your first account at launch when no startup account is saved, and Calendar, To Do and People launches open the welcome page when no account is set up.
- The taskbar jump list no longer fails to update at startup.
