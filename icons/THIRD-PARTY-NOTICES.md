# Third-party icon notices

The WinoIcons fonts other than Classic are built from these packages (pinned in `sets.json`). Their outlines are
redrawn onto the WinoIcons grid; brand marks in every font are the Classic artwork.

| Set | Package | Version | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Material Rounded | [@material-symbols/svg-400](https://github.com/marella/material-symbols) (Material Symbols by Google) | 0.47.6 | Apache License 2.0, Copyright Google LLC |
| Framework7 | [framework7-icons](https://github.com/framework7io/framework7-icons) | 5.0.5 | MIT, Copyright (c) 2016 Vladimir Kharlampidi |

The full license texts ship inside each package tarball (`LICENSE`), which `import_icon_sets.py` caches in `icons/.cache`.
